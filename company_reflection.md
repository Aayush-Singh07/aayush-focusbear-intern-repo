# Company Reflection — Aayush Kumar Singh

## Understanding Focus Bear's Mission & Vision

### Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
It was made specifically for people with ADHD and AuDHD, to help them focus on their academic, professional, and personal work.

### What problem is Focus Bear solving?
It's helping these individuals focus longer and work without burnout, and understand their special needs.

### Why do you think this mission is important?
This isn't just another focus app. These individuals have a higher dropout rate and struggle in their personal and professional lives, which in many cases can lead to suicide. So it's really a big cause.

### How does Focus Bear's work align with your personal values or interests?
I myself feel that I have traits related to ADHD, and I understand the severe challenges people with it face.

### Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
Yes — focusing in long work sessions, and burnout.
