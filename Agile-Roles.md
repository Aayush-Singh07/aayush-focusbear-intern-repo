# Roles in an Agile Team — Aayush Kumar Singh

## Research & Learn

**Key roles in an Agile team:**
- **Developers:** build and maintain the actual product — writing code, fixing bugs, reviewing others' PRs, and ensuring what ships actually works.
- **QA (Quality Assurance):** test features before and after release, catch bugs developers might miss, write and run test cases, and help define what "done" actually means for a feature.
- **Product Manager (PM):** decides what gets built and why — prioritizes the backlog based on user needs and business goals, writes requirements/specs, and is the bridge between user problems and technical solutions.
- **Agile Project Manager (Agile PM):** focuses on how work flows — removing blockers, facilitating ceremonies (standups, retros), tracking velocity/progress, and keeping the team's process healthy rather than deciding what to build.
- **Other supporting roles:** UX/UI designers shape how features look and feel before/during development; stakeholders (e.g., CEO, business ops) provide direction and priorities; customer support surfaces real user pain points back into the backlog.

**How these roles collaborate in Kanban vs. Scrum:**
In Scrum, roles collaborate around fixed sprint ceremonies — planning together at the start, syncing daily, reviewing/retro-ing at the end, all as a tightly synced unit. In Kanban, collaboration is more continuous and async — a developer picks up a card, tags QA or the PM as needed at any point (not just at sprint boundaries), and the board itself (not a sprint meeting) is the shared source of truth for where everything stands.

## Reflection

**My role, and how it contributes to the Agile process:**
As a Mobile App Developer Intern, I contribute by picking up issues from the board, building/fixing features in the React Native codebase, and pushing work through review before it's marked done. My contribution is concrete and visible — a feature either works and passes review, or it doesn't — which fits well with Kanban's "keep the flow moving" model.

**How my responsibilities overlap with other roles:**
I overlap most with QA (my code needs to be tested before it's genuinely "done") and with whoever reviews my PRs (senior mobile developers) for code quality and architectural fit. I also indirectly connect to the PM/product side, since understanding *why* a feature matters (not just the ticket description) helps me make better small decisions while building it.

## Task

**How my role fits into the Agile team:**
I sit in the "build it" part of the flow — I take a defined task from the board, implement it in React Native, test it myself before marking it ready for review, and respond to review feedback until it's merged. I'm not deciding priorities, but how well I execute directly affects how smoothly the team's overall flow works.

**One role I frequently collaborate with, and how to improve teamwork:**
QA is the role I'll most frequently hand off to. One way to improve that collaboration: writing clearer, more specific PR descriptions (what changed, how to test it, any edge cases I already considered) so QA doesn't have to guess at scope or re-discover context I already have — this saves back-and-forth and speeds up the whole review cycle.
