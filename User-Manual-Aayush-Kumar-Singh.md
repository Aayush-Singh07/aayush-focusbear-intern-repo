# User Manual for Aayush Kumar Singh

## Research & Learn (Notes)
A "User Manual for Me" is a short doc that explains how you work best — communication style, focus habits, feedback preferences — so teammates don't have to guess or learn it the slow way through trial and error. Teams use them because most friction in collaboration comes from mismatched assumptions (e.g., someone expects instant replies while the other person does async deep-work blocks), not from actual disagreement. Writing it down up front saves everyone time and avoids misreading normal working-style differences as a problem.

## What I Need From Teammates
- **Direct, specific feedback** — tell me plainly if something's wrong or won't work, rather than softening it so much I miss the actual point. I'd rather fix a real problem early than get polished praise on something broken.
- Clear task scope up front — I move fast once I understand the goal, so a clear "what" and "why" gets more out of me than a vague ask I have to reverse-engineer.
- Room to figure things out myself first — I'll ask when I'm genuinely stuck, but I default to trying, researching, and testing assumptions before asking for help.

## How I Prefer to Communicate
- Async messages for most day-to-day updates and questions — I can respond quickly and it doesn't interrupt deep work.
- Short, clear standups — I'll give a quick, structured update (what I did, what's next, any blockers) rather than a long narrative.
- For anything genuinely complex or ambiguous, a quick call beats a long back-and-forth thread — faster to resolve live.

## What Helps Me Stay Focused and Productive
- Working in focused blocks rather than constant context-switching
- A clear task/issue to work against (I work well against a defined checklist, like this onboarding repo's issue structure)
- Minimal unnecessary process — I'd rather spend time building/fixing than in overly long meetings

## How I Like to Give and Receive Feedback
- Clear and direct, without excessive softening — I take feedback well and would rather know exactly what to fix.
- I try to give feedback the same way I want to receive it: specific, actionable, and honest, even if it's not what someone wants to hear.
- Written feedback (PR comments, issue comments) works well for me since I can reference it later; verbal is fine for anything urgent or nuanced.

## Quirks / Things to Know About My Work Style
- I'm currently balancing this internship with other commitments (coursework, another internship, exam prep), so my working hours are concentrated in defined blocks rather than spread evenly through the day — I've documented this in my time plan.
- I learn fastest by actually doing the task hands-on rather than just reading about it or watching a tutorial — expect me to dig into the real codebase quickly rather than staying purely theoretical.
- I ask a lot of "why" questions when something seems off — not to push back for its own sake, but because I'd rather understand the reasoning than follow instructions blindly.
- I catch my own mistakes better when I slow down and double-check before submitting — so if something feels rushed, I'll flag it rather than ship it half-checked.
