## Research Notes (reference material — pulled from public comparison articles, for your own reference while writing your answers below)

**Freedom** — Cross-platform distraction blocker (blocks apps/websites across devices in sync). Strength: hardcore blocking, works across all your devices at once. Weakness (commonly noted): blocking alone doesn't solve task initiation — you can be fully blocked and still not start the task, since the barrier for ADHD is often starting, not distraction access.

**Cold Turkey** — Similar hardcore blocker, primarily Mac/Windows-focused, known for being very difficult to bypass once a block is set (a strength for willpower-proof blocking, but rigid).

**Tiimo** — Visual daily planner built specifically for neurodivergent brains; color-coded timeline view, AI task breakdown, body-doubling support, widgets, Apple Watch app. Named Apple's iPhone App of the Year 2025. Strength: makes time visible/concrete for time-blind brains. Weakness (commonly noted): it's a planner, not a blocker or focus-execution tool — doesn't stop you from getting distracted once your day is planned.

**Routinery** — Focused specifically on sequenced routines (morning/evening/workout) with timers and audio cues walking you through each step. Strength: very strong for recurring routines specifically. Weakness: less flexible for ad-hoc, non-routine tasks compared to a general planner.

**General pattern across the market:** most competitors solve ONE piece of the ADHD executive-function puzzle (blocking OR planning OR routines) rather than the whole loop. This seems to be the space Focus Bear is positioned in — real-time support across the actual moment of struggle, not just one static feature.
